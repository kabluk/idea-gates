# Arvid Kahl — *The Embedded Entrepreneur* (2021)

Audience-first: find a community, understand its problems, then build.

## Ideas we use

- **Pick an audience before a product.** A group that gathers somewhere
  (forums, subreddits, Slack/Discord groups, trade associations) and talks
  about its work is where problems become visible.
  → "Go where people complain" in the hunt.
- **Problems show up as patterns in conversations**: repeated questions,
  repeated frustrations, the same workaround recommended again and again.
  → One post is an anecdote; a repeated pattern across threads is evidence.
- **Embed and contribute before selling.** Trust is earned in the
  community over time.
  → For a founder without trust assets, this is a *cost* to put in gate 2
  and gate 5, not a free channel: "join the community and become known" is a
  months-long plan, not a first step.

## Limits

The method assumes the founder will participate publicly. With "no public
face" in `FOUNDER.md`, the scout uses communities for evidence only and must
find a channel that does not need the founder's reputation.
