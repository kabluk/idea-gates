# W. Chan Kim, Renée Mauborgne — *Blue Ocean Strategy* (2005)

Create uncontested market space instead of fighting over an existing one.

## Ideas we use

- **Value innovation**: raise value for the buyer and lower cost at the
  same time, by questioning which factors the industry competes on.
- **The four actions (ERRC grid)**: which factors to *eliminate*, *reduce*
  below the standard, *raise* above it, and *create* that the industry has
  never offered. → The scout's reframing step for crowded jobs.
- **Strategy canvas**: plot the offers on the factors buyers care about;
  a new offer must have a visibly different shape, not a slightly better
  version of the same curve. → A card whose product is "like X but cheaper"
  is a red ocean card.
- **Non-customers**: look at people who refuse or never consider the
  industry's offer. → Overlaps with non-consumption in jobs-to-be-done.

## Limits

The book's examples are large companies redefining industries. For a solo
founder, the useful version is narrow: an "ocean" is often just a results
page without a free interactive tool yet, in a language incumbents ignore.
The skill's Phase 2 measures that with data rather than canvases.
