# Payout reconciliation for Mercado Livre sellers in Brazil

- Customer: small Mercado Livre sellers in Brazil (~300 orders/month).
- Job: when payouts arrive, make sure fees and refunds were charged correctly; today they trust the statement or check by hand in a spreadsheet.
- Evidence: about 24K searches/month around marketplace fees in Brazil; a winnable results page.
- Product: connect the seller account via OAuth, find leakage, help claim it back.
- Price idea: 20% of recovered money.
- Why now: no specific event.
- First physical step: a seller who does not know the founder grants OAuth access to their account finances.
- Load-bearing number: how much the marketplace underpays a typical seller.
