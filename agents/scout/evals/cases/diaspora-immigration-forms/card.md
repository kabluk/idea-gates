# Russian-language help with Spanish immigration paperwork

- Customer: Russian-speaking newcomers in Spain applying for residence permits.
- Job: when filling in official forms, get them right the first time; today they ask in chat groups or pay a gestor.
- Evidence: recurring questions about specific forms in Russian-language expat groups; Russian-language search volume for form names.
- Product: a guided web wizard that tells the user which permit to apply for in their situation and fills in the forms.
- Price idea: €29 per application.
- Why now: steady inflow of newcomers.
- First physical step: a newcomer finds the wizard through search and pays by card.
- Load-bearing number: conversion from form-name searches to paid wizard runs.
