# Education products from a video

The education lens of the brief: what this video's know-how could become as a
workshop, a niche course or a big programme — for this user, at prices the
market actually pays. Adapted from an "education product line" prompt the user
supplied (10 ideas in three price tiers, each with a name); the tiers are kept,
the fixed prices are replaced by a market check, and the rules below are added
because an education product lives or dies on trust, not on the idea.

## Rule zero: teach what you have done

Nobody buys a course from someone who watched a video. An education product
is the author's **own result** packaged — so every idea here is gated on the
user first doing the instruction from the brief and having a case (numbers,
screenshots, a client). Write each idea with "what case you need first". An
idea that needs a case the user cannot get in 30 days goes to "later".

## Rule one: courses sell through an audience, not through search

Measured 2026-10-17: Google searches for "ai agents course", "claude code
course", "vibe coding course" — zero a month in US/UK/DE; "курс ии агенты",
"курс claude code" — noise in the KZ/UZ/UA proxy. Meanwhile there are 29
Claude courses from 16 Russian schools (checkroi.ru, July 2026). People buy
courses from authors they already follow. So every education idea comes with
its channel: which posts (from the public drafts) build the audience that will
buy it, and the free lead magnet that turns a reader into a subscriber.

## The ladder

Always propose a ladder, not a list — each step is the funnel into the next:

| Step | What | Typical price (check!) | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Lead magnet | free guide / checklist / template from the brief's instruction | free | the instruction, done once |
| 1. Workshop | 1.5–3 hours live or recorded, one concrete result by the end | RU 2–7K ₽; EN $50–150 | one own case |
| 2. Niche course | 2–4 weeks, a narrow skill for a narrow audience | RU 15–40K ₽; EN $200–500 | 3+ cases, 20+ workshop buyers |
| 3. Big programme / cohort | 6–12 weeks, a transformation (new profession, a product launched) | RU 50–150K ₽; EN $1,000–2,500 | a track record and an audience |

Price bands are a starting point from October 2026 data (checkroi.ru median
for Claude courses ~60K ₽, range 749 ₽–192K ₽; Maven AI cohorts reported at
$1,500–2,500 by a third-party review — unverified). For each idea, check
comparable offers with web search (`checkroi.ru <тема>`, `site:maven.com
<topic>`, `site:udemy.com <topic>`, `site:stepik.org <тема>`) and write the
comparable you found next to the price.

## What to write for each idea (5–10 ideas total, across the tiers)

- **Name** — specific and unusual; avoid the generic ("ChatGPT for
  beginners"). Name the result, not the tool.
- **For whom** — one concrete person ("a freelance marketer who already uses
  ChatGPT but not agents").
- **Result** — what they can do at the end that they could not before.
- **Programme** — 3–6 bullets, built from the brief's instruction and the
  "between the lines" section (the speaker's real workflow details are the
  most valuable course material).
- **Price** — with the comparable offer it is based on.
- **Case you need first** — and whether it is reachable in 30 days.
- **Shelf life** — tool-specific courses (one CLI, one product) age in months;
  method courses (verification, planning with agents) last years. Prefer
  method, use the tool as the example.

Mark the single best first step: almost always the lead magnet plus one
workshop built on the user's own first case.

## Do not

- Put third-party promotion (someone's channel, affiliate links) into the
  ideas or the posts.
- Promise income results ("earn 100K in a month") in names or programmes.
- Propose a big programme before the workshop has buyers.
